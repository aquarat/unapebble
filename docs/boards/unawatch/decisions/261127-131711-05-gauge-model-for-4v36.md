# The gauge model back to the stock firmware's, for a 4.4 V cell

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-27 13:17 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
261126-185344-02-cap-through-the-charge-voltage set the 4.20 V cap through
the battery's charge voltage, so the gauge model followed: ModelCFG.VChg = 0
(ModelCFG 0x0000), which differs from the stock firmware's model and made
each firmware reload its own after a switch, losing what the gauge learned.
With the charge at 4.36 V (261127-131711-04-charge-to-4v36), the model the
driver derives from the devicetree is the stock firmware's again. Supersedes
261126-185344-02-cap-through-the-charge-voltage.

## Decision
The charge voltage stays the battery's devicetree property, not the limit
option, and the model follows it as before: VChg = 1 for a charge above
4.25 V, ModelCFG 0x0400, FullSOCThr 80%, every model register equal to the
stock firmware's (261126-173552-02-fuel-gauge-model, checked by
`tests/drivers/gauge/test_max17262.c`). The end of a 4.36 V charge is the
gauge's full, and it learns the capacity to 4.36 V.

## Consequences
Switching between the stock firmware and PebbleOS reloads no model, so the
learning survives. On the first boot after the 4.20 V builds the gauge
refreshes its model (VChg changes), keeping what it learned, and converges on
the new full over a cycle or two. With the limit option set, FullSOCThr
differs and the stock firmware reloads its model, as before.

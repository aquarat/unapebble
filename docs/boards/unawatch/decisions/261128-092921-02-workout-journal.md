# Workouts recorded as an append-only journal of 32-byte records

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-28 09:29 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
261128-092921-01 makes the Sport app the watch's recorder. Its recorder
writes 24-byte records without a time of their own, buffers 30 s in the
app's heap and writes the activity's only summary at the end: a reset, an
app fault or a power loss loses up to 30 s, and leaves segments marked as
still recording with no summary, which nothing reads back. PFS files have a
fixed size, set when created, and read 0xff where not written; writes
program in place (bits cleared, as on NOR). On the eMMC each 512-byte block
is written with a reliable write, so a power loss leaves a block old or new
(261126-172141-04). The eMMC powers down after 2 s idle and its first write
after power-up takes 110 to 125 ms, later ones 2 to 4 ms (261127-130125-03).

## Decision
- An activity is the journal `/wk<start>.<n>`, `<start>` the RTC's UTC start
  in 8 hex digits, `<n>` from 0: segments of a 32-byte header and 1024
  records of 32 bytes (17 min at 1 Hz), created at their full size and
  filled in order. A record or header never straddles a 512-byte block, so
  each lands whole or not at all; each also ends with a CRC-16, so a reader
  stops at the first erased record and skips a damaged one.
- Records are typed: a *sample* each second (time since the start, position,
  cumulative distance, speed, elevation from the barometer calibrated on the
  GNSS or from the GNSS, cadence, heart rate and power when present,
  satellites, HDOP, state flags), an *event* (start, pause, resume,
  auto-pause, auto-resume, lap with the lap's statistics, stop with the
  totals, resumed after a restart) and a *time* record (the GNSS's UTC and
  the RTC's at the same second, 261128-092921-05). Everything a summary or a
  FIT file needs is in the journal; nothing is rewritten.
- `/wk<start>.s` is a summary written at the stop, a cache for listing; an
  activity without one is unfinished.
- Records are buffered in the recorder's static RAM, not the app's heap, and
  written every 10 s (`CONFIG_APP_SPORT_FLUSH_S`), at once for each event,
  and from the restart and power-off handlers, so an orderly restart or the
  low-battery power-off loses nothing and a fault, watchdog reset or sudden
  power loss at most the last 10 s.
- An unfinished activity found when a sport is opened is offered for
  resuming (its totals and laps rebuilt from the journal, paused, the gap
  marked with an event) if it is of that sport and its last record is under
  30 min old; otherwise, and when declined, it is closed: a stop event with
  totals computed from the samples, then the summary, marked recovered.
- Headers carry a magic, a format version and the record size. Readers skip
  activities of a version they do not know, without deleting them. The
  names differ from the earlier recorder's `/act...`, which never ran on the
  watch.
- Retention: the 30 newest activities (`CONFIG_APP_SPORT_MAX_ACTIVITIES`).
  Before a segment is created, the oldest finished activities are deleted
  while the filesystem has less than the segment and 1 MiB free; if that is
  not enough, the recording ends there and the activity is marked truncated.
  An activity ends after 24 h.

## Consequences
About 115 KiB an hour, 8 eMMC writes a minute while recording (a power-up
and a write every 10 s, measured in the board page's Power section). A
reboot mid-workout keeps what was written and, from the app, carries on the
same activity. Firmware without the Sport app, or of an older format, leaves
the files alone. The FIT encoder and the export read the journal only.

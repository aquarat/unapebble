# Keep the charge safety timers at 5 h and 30 minutes

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26 17:35 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
The PCA9420 suspends a charge that runs too long: a pre-qualification timer
and a fast charge timer (CHG_TIMER_EN). The stock firmware sets the fast timer
to its maximum, 9 h, "to avoid premature termination for deeply discharged
batteries".

## Decision
Timers on, fast charge 5 h (twice the 2.5 h a full charge at 100 mA should
take), pre-qualification 30 min, not doubled (TIMER_2X off). The PCA9420
doubles them itself while in thermal regulation.

## Consequences
A cell that does not take its charge (failing, or a short) is cut off earlier
than with the stock firmware. A timer that expires stops the charge until the
cable is plugged in again.

# Load the stock firmware's EZ model, only when the gauge lost it

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26 17:35 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
The MAX17262 learns the cell (capacity, resistance, cycles) as long as the
battery powers it, which is all the time but after a battery removal or a
complete discharge, when it comes back from a power-on reset (Status.POR)
with defaults. The stock firmware checks its model registers at every boot
and reloads the model on any mismatch, wiping what was learned
(`FuelGauge.cpp`, `MAX17262.cpp`). The owner may switch between the stock
firmware and PebbleOS.

## Decision
The same EZ model as the stock firmware, from the devicetree: DesignCap 200
mAh (0x0190), IChgTerm 14 mA (0x0059), VEmpty 3.40 V / recovery 3.96 V
(0xAA63), FullSOCThr 80% (0x5005, Maxim's EZ recommendation), SOCHold 0x1000
(99% hold on, empty hold off) and ModelCFG.VChg = 1 for a cell charged above
4.25 V (0x0400). The model is loaded, following UG6595 (wait for FStat.DNR,
leave hibernate, write, ModelCFG.Refresh, restore HibCFG), only when Status.POR
is set or a register differs; then POR is cleared. Unlike the stock firmware,
a mismatch without a power-on reset does not reset the learned registers: the
refresh keeps them and the gauge converges again. Every reading checks POR and
DesignCap and configures the gauge again when either says it reset.

## Consequences
Switching firmware does not make either one reload the model, so the learning
survives (with CHARGER_PCA9420_CHARGE_LIMIT_MV set, FullSOCThr differs and the
stock firmware reloads it). Checked against the stock encoders in
`tests/drivers/gauge/test_max17262.c`; not yet on a watch.

# A BMM350 driver that polls, with the SensorAPI's compensation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-27 19:48 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
The BMM350 magnetometer is on I2C4 at `0x14`, its interrupt on PC2. Its raw
readings mean little alone: each part carries its calibration (offsets,
sensitivities, their temperature coefficients, cross-axis terms) in an OTP,
which Bosch's BMM350 SensorAPI reads at init and applies to every reading.
The stock firmware uses the SensorAPI (with three changes of its own: the
sensor time read with the data, and a bound on the OTP poll), and found the
part's mounting on a watch (MagBosch.cpp:20-29). PebbleOS's magnetometer
class wants a sample in milligauss on the watch's axes, at the interval the
compass service asks, or none.

## Decision
`drivers/imu/bmm350` is PebbleOS's own driver; the compensation in
`bmm350_regs.h` is the SensorAPI's (v1.4.0, BSD-3-Clause, credited in NOTICE),
its OTP layout, nominal gains and formulas ported to C as they are, unit
tested. At init it resets the part, reads the 32 OTP words (the poll bounded
as the stock firmware bounds it) and powers the OTP off. The part stays
suspended unless sampled; a sampling interval starts it with a magnetic
reset (BR then FGR, as the SensorAPI does at init), then its normal mode at
the output data rate with the longest period no longer than asked, averaging
4 samples as far as that rate allows (`CONFIG_MAG_BMM350_AVG_LOG2`). A
delayed work item reads it at that rate, so the CPU sleeps in between. The
interrupt line is not used. The mount matrix is the stock firmware's.

## Consequences
The compass service (`CONFIG_MAG`, which enables it) runs on the part.
`imu mag read` in the shell samples it once. Hard-iron offsets of the watch
itself, which the compass's calibration takes out, are not handled by the
driver: the first readings on a watch were about 100 uT in all, twice the
Earth's field.

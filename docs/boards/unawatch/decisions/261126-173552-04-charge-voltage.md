# Charge to 4.36 V

- Status: Superseded by 261126-185344-01-charge-to-4v20
- Date: 2026-11-26 17:35 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
The stock firmware charges to 4.40 V (`main.h:248`, "Max 4500!"), a
high-voltage lithium-polymer cell; the cell's data sheet is not in the
sources. The PCA9420's VBAT_REG is accurate to 0.75%
(data sheet Rev. 3.1, electrical characteristics), so 4.40 V may regulate at up to 4.433 V. The
time a lithium cell spends near its top voltage is the main cause of its
ageing; each 0.1 V less roughly doubles its cycle life for a few percent of
capacity.

## Decision
`constant-charge-voltage-max-microvolt = <4360000>`: VBAT_REG 4.36 V (code
0x26). With the worst-case 0.75% it stays at or below 4.393 V, under the 4.40 V
the stock firmware treats as the cell's charge voltage. The encoding rounds
down, so no configuration can exceed what the devicetree asks.

## Consequences
About 3 to 5% less capacity than the stock firmware, and less wear per cycle.
The gauge model keeps VChg = 1 (4.3 to 4.4 V cell).

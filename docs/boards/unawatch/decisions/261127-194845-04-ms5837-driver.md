# An MS5837 driver that owns the barometer's LDO, through a regulator

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-27 19:48 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
The MS5837-02BA01 barometer is on I2C2 at `0x76`, powered by an AP7350 LDO
whose enable, ALTIMETER_EN on PG0, must always be driven; the stock firmware
switches it off for 20 ms, then on and waits 10 ms (BaroMs5837.cpp:554-558).
PebbleOS's board code drove PG0 low at boot, after the devices. Read on the
watch with PG0 high: the part answers, its PROM C0 `0x7001` (CRC 7, version 0,
the 02BA01) and its CRC checks. PebbleOS's pressure class runs one
conversion a read, on demand: the `pressure` shell command, the pressure
service and the sport app, which is off on this board.

## Decision
PG0 is a `regulator-fixed` in the board's devicetree, active high, 10 ms to
come up, and the barometer's `vdd-supply`; the board no longer drives it.
`drivers/pressure/ms5837.c` powers it at init (off for 20 ms, on), resets the
part and checks its PROM: the CRC, a version of the 02BA (02BA01 or 02BA21,
which share the compensation) and not all zero, as the stock firmware checks.
The supply then stays on: the part takes under a microamp in standby, and
powering it for each read would add 30 ms to it. A read converts the
pressure, then the temperature, at OSR 4096 (`CONFIG_PRESSURE_MS5837_OSR`,
9 ms each) and compensates them to the first and second order of the 02BA's
data sheet, in which 0.01 mbar is a pascal. A read that fails powers the part
again once and tries again.

## Consequences
`CONFIG_PRESSURE` is on for the watch, with the `pressure` shell command.
A board without the regulator can give the driver no supply. The LDO's own
quiescent current, which the gauge cannot resolve, is spent all the time.

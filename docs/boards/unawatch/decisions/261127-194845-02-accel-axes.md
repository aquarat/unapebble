# The accelerometer's axes: PebbleOS's, as the gravity vector

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-27 19:48 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
PebbleOS's axes are X to the right of the watch (3 o'clock), Y to its top
(12 o'clock) and Z out of the face (`pbl/services/imu/units.h`), and a watch
lying still face up reads z = -1000 mg (the simulator, and its sample's
documentation): the readings are the gravity vector, the negative of what an
accelerometer measures. The stock firmware's frame has the same axes, with
the accelerometer's own sign (MagBosch.cpp:20-24), and remaps the BMI270's
axes for its feature engine as x = -Y, y = X, z = Z (BMI270.cpp:684-689),
which Bosch's SensorAPI applies to the features only, not to the data
registers. On the watch, lying still (face up as far as could be seen from
afar), the part's Z read +1 g.

## Decision
The board's mount matrix gives PebbleOS's readings from the part's:
x = Y, y = -X, z = -Z, the stock firmware's remap negated. The BMM350's
matrix is the stock firmware's, measured on a watch, since a field is not
negated.

## Consequences
On the watch lying still, z reads -1009 mg, x and y within 50 mg of 0. The
X and Y signs rest on the stock firmware's remap: a watch tilted with 3
o'clock down should read x = +1000, 12 o'clock down y = +1000. That needs
someone to move the watch.

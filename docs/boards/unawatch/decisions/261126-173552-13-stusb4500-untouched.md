# Leave the STUSB4500 alone

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26 17:35 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
The STUSB4500 runs on its factory NVM; the stock firmware never programs
PDOs, only masks its alerts, reads its status for debug accessory and fault
detection, and resets it on faults (`UsbProtection.cpp`). It is powered from
VBUS, dies at unplug and, read at that moment, can hold the shared I2C bus
low (`Power.cpp:199-206`). Its ALERT shares PWR_INT_L (PG7) with the PMIC.

## Decision
PebbleOS does not talk to it: no PDO or NVM writes, no alert masking. The PMIC
interrupt takes both edges of PG7 and the battery service polls, so an ALERT
the STUSB4500 holds low while USB is in does not hide a plug or unplug for
more than one poll.

## Consequences
The charger gets the 5 V the factory NVM negotiates, as with the stock
firmware. No debug accessory or USB fault detection; the console's USB_DAM
switch stays as `CONFIG_BOARD_UNAWATCH_CONSOLE_DAM` sets it. A USB driver may
need it later.

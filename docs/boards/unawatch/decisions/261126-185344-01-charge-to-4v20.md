# Charge to no more than 4.20 V until the cell is identified

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26 18:53 UTC
- Decided by: owner

## Context
The cell's data sheet is not in UNA's sources, which give 200 mAh in one place
and 280 mAh in another, and the stock firmware charges it to 4.40 V, which
suits only a high-voltage cell. Supersedes
261126-173552-04-charge-voltage (4.36 V), which assumed a high-voltage cell.

## Decision
PebbleOS charges the UNA Watch to at most 4.20 V, the charge voltage every
lithium-ion and lithium-polymer cell takes, until the cell is identified.

## Consequences
If the cell is a high-voltage one, about 10 to 15% of its capacity is left
unused, and it ages more slowly. The battery capacity test measures the
capacity at this voltage. The stock firmware, when it runs, still charges to
4.40 V.

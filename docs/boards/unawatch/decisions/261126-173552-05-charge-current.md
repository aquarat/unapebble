# Charge at 100 mA, 0.5C

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26 17:35 UTC
- Decided by: agent

## Context
The stock firmware charges the 200 mAh cell at 150 mA (0.75C, `main.h:242`);
its own fallback is 100 mA (`Pmic.cpp:21`). Small lithium-polymer cells are
usually specified for a standard charge of 0.5C and at most 1C; a lower
current heats the cell and the linear charger less.

## Decision
`constant-charge-current-max-microamp = <100000>`: ICHG_CC 100 mA (code 0x14).
The driver refuses a charge current above 1C of the battery's capacity.

## Consequences
A full charge takes about 2.5 h instead of about 1.7 h. If the cell turns out
larger than 200 mAh (a stock firmware comment mentions 280 mAh), the C-rate is
lower still.
